# The five tasks

Replace **[language]** with Yoruba, Hausa or Igbo, whichever you chose. Use
the same language for every task, on both models. Start a new chat for each
task.

**1. Translate**
> Translate into [language]: "Wash your hands with soap before you eat and after you use the toilet. Boil drinking water or treat it before use. If a child has diarrhoea for more than two days, take the child to the clinic."

**2. Write a message**
> Write a short WhatsApp message in [language] inviting my neighbour to my daughter's naming ceremony on Saturday at 10 a.m.

**3. Explain**
> In [language], explain in three or four sentences why children should sleep under a mosquito net.

**4. Advise**
> Answer in [language]: what should a small farmer do to prepare before the rainy season starts?

**5. Summarise**
> Summarise this in [language] in two sentences: "The community borehole was repaired last week after three months out of service. Women and children no longer walk two hours to the river for water. The water committee will now collect a small monthly fee from each household to pay for future repairs, and a mechanic from the next town has agreed to train two young people from the community."
